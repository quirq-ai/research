## What this topic is

qq is modelled on Chromium's infrastructure (depot_tools, gclient, recipes,
LUCI, Sheriff-o-Matic, AutoRoll and release channels), rebuilt for small
GitHub repos. Its parts live in 13 repos under
[quirq-ai](https://github.com/quirq-ai), and two product repos, innernet
(Next.js) and xo-space (Python), use it. Reading 13 READMEs is a slow way to
learn how the parts connect, so this topic maps them in one interactive app,
with a page for each repo. Purpose and research action live in
[GOAL.md](GOAL.md).

## Research and findings so far

Each finding was checked against the repos at the commits listed in the app's
footer on 2026-10-05.

- **The 13 repos fall into four stages: your machine (depot, sync, recipes,
  toolchains, remote-build), before landing (infra-config, gate,
  test-pipelines), after landing (gardener, rollers, perf) and shipping
  (release, installer).** Source: each repo's README. Verified: 2026-10-05.
- **A product repo carries only a manifest, `infra/repo.toml`, plus CI
  workflows generated by infra-config; sync is the only code that reads or
  edits that manifest.** Source:
  [sync README](https://github.com/quirq-ai/sync),
  [infra-config README](https://github.com/quirq-ai/infra-config).
  Verified: 2026-10-05.
- **Toolchains are published for Linux x86_64 only, so on macOS `qq sync`
  stops at the toolchain step.** Source:
  [toolchains](https://github.com/quirq-ai/toolchains). Verified: 2026-10-05.
- **Retry-then-compare-with-base exists in test-pipelines but is not switched
  on for the product repos; a required check fails on the first failing
  test.** Source: [test-pipelines](https://github.com/quirq-ai/test-pipelines).
  Verified: 2026-10-05.
- **gardener opens revert PRs within a cap of 10 a day but lands none on its
  own: its auto-land list is empty.** Source:
  [gardener](https://github.com/quirq-ai/gardener). Verified: 2026-10-05.
- **release and installer are built, but no channel has shipped yet.**
  Source: [release README](https://github.com/quirq-ai/release). Verified:
  2026-10-05.
