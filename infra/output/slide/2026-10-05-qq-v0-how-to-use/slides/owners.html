OWNERS AND BOTS
Who decides, who acts
People
suraj is the policy owner: policy paths, stable promotion and repo settings.
org.toml names suraj owner of the org and all 13 infra repos (infra-config 310e326). Other config areas and the product repos in repos.toml still have empty owners lists (V0-ORG-02).
Every infra repo's CODEOWNERS names suraj on its policy and trust paths, with no catch-all.
qqcfg validate [--todos]
lists every empty owner list and rotation; --todos adds all open TODOs, 3 of them for v0.
Agents and bots
Agents may land alone only clean reverts, dependency rolls and docs, and promote to canary.
One GitHub App per tool. Live: quirqer (rollers). After v0, designed with suraj: the gardener App and the release executor.
A bot works only in repos whose owner-bot property names it.
GitHub does not let a PR's author approve it: suraj can approve agent PRs (opened as quirq-gates), but suraj's own PRs need a second owner or a bot.
