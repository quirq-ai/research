# Goal: design

## Purpose

Decide how quirq should design its product surfaces (product UI, docs and the
research hub) so they look and feel consistent. It informs the design choices
behind those surfaces.

It also covers motion design: how motion graphics and explainer videos are
made with Claude, learned from the public Prompt Motion gallery, and applied
to turn a piece of research into a video whose every on-screen claim is
measured.

## Research questions

1. Which design system or component library fits quirq's stack?
2. Which design principles and tokens should quirq adopt (type, colour,
   spacing, dark mode)?
3. How do comparable developer-tool companies handle design?
4. How are motion graphics made with Claude, and what makes a prompt for them
   work? Can the method present a research result without losing accuracy?

## Research action

Review 4 to 6 public design systems, from their official documentation and
source, and compare them against quirq's needs.

For motion design: read the Prompt Motion gallery and its analyses, count what
its entries say with a reproducible script, and apply the method to one
research post, measuring every claim the video shows.

## Scope

**In scope:**

- Design of quirq's product UI, docs and the research hub.
- Public design systems and component libraries.
- Motion graphics and explainer videos made with Claude, as the Prompt Motion
  gallery documents them, and one worked example.

**Out of scope:**

- System or architecture design.

## Done when

- Each research question has a sourced answer.
- The onepager and the report are published.
- The motion research is a report and a one-pager, and the worked example is
  an app with its source.
