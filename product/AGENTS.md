# Agent instructions: product

Read the root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md) first. This file adds instructions for
this topic only.

## What to research

Derived from [GOAL.md](GOAL.md), in this order:

1. Publish the essay *What is a unit of work?* as a report, with its figures.
2. Publish the whitepaper *quirq: A Unit of Work for Intelligence* as its PDF,
   unchanged.
3. Publish a one-pager with the key numbers, the open questions and links into
   both.

## Sources

**Prefer:** suraj's posts in
[sharmasuraj0123/writings](https://github.com/sharmasuraj0123/writings), read
at a recorded commit; the whitepaper's full text, `llm.txt`, at the same
commit; and the primary sources those posts cite.

**Avoid:** describing the quirq product or its calculus from memory or from
later drafts. The essay and the whitepaper describe them as of July and August
2026.

## How to verify

- Every published piece names its original post, links it at a recorded
  commit, and credits suraj as the author.
- Bring text across without changing its meaning: keep every claim, number,
  table and figure, and keep the source's labels: sourced, derived, measured
  in mock mode, illustrative, open.
- Check a whitepaper claim against `llm.txt`, not against the PDF's layout.
- Where the essay and the whitepaper disagree, keep both texts unchanged and
  point to the correction. Do not quote either figure as settled.

## Publishing

Follow steps 5 to 8 of the root [AGENTS.md](../AGENTS.md): publish only into
the formats ticked in [GOAL.md](GOAL.md), then update [README.md](README.md)
and run `npm run topics` at the repo root.
